Roastr Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Roastr ("the app") is made by @joshipraneel on GitHub. It roasts you into putting your phone down. This policy explains what the app handles and why. The short version: everything stays on your phone. Roastr has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and does not connect to the internet.

Accessibility service

Reel counting is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, Roastr's Accessibility service only receives events from YouTube and Instagram. It uses the ID of the screen that is showing to tell when you swipe to the next reel. It does not read what you watch, type or message, and it does not collect or share any of it. When you pass your limit it presses Back and Home to send you out of the app. You can turn it off any time in your phone's Accessibility settings, or use "Count minutes" instead, which does not need Accessibility.

Sharing

Roastr does not send any data to the developer or to anyone else. The only time anything leaves the app is when you tap "Share" on a shame card: the card image goes to the app you pick, like any other share.

Backups

If Android backup is on for your phone, your settings and daily stats may be included in your own Google backup, as with any app. Voice recordings are never included.

Deleting your data

Uninstall Roastr, or go to Settings > Apps > Roastr > Storage > Clear data. That removes everything. Since nothing is sent to us, there is nothing else to delete.

Age

Roastr is for adults (18+). Roasts include strong language, and the strongest level asks you to confirm you are 18 or older.
