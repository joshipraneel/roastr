What it does

Two jobs: get you off your phone at night, and out of bed in the morning.

Doomscroll police

Set a daily limit for YouTube and Instagram. Pass it and you get roasted, again every five minutes you stay.

Reel counter

Optional: count every Short and Reel you swipe, with a pill in the corner that goes green, yellow, red. At the limit you're sent home.

Wake-up roast

An alarm that yells you out of bed. Make it stop with three maths sums or 30 real steps.

Score and streak

A daily score out of 100. Behave and the roasts go easy on you. Don't and you'll hear about it.

Shame card and your week

Your numbers plus a fresh roast, ready to share. Plus a weekly summary with the title you earned.

Four languages

English, हिंदी, मराठी and ಕನ್ನಡ, voiced. Three spice levels, from grandma-safe to 18+. Or record your own.

Private by design

Roastr doesn't even have internet permission.

Stays on your phone

Your name, stats and recordings never leave the device. No account, no analytics, no ads.

Counts, doesn't read

Minutes mode only sees how long apps were open. Reel mode only listens to YouTube and Instagram to count swipes, and asks first.

Delete anytime

Uninstall or clear the app's storage and everything is gone. Full privacy policy.

Install

  1. Download roastr.apk on your Android phone.
  2. Open it. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps.
  3. Open Roastr and let it insult you through setup.
